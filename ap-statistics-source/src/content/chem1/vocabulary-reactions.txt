### Decomposition reaction
- Definition: ==One reactant forms two or more products==.
- Relation: \(AB\rightarrow A+B\).
- Visual: reaction
### Precipitation reaction
- Definition: Dissolved ions form an ==insoluble ionic solid==.
- Key idea: A solid alone does not prove precipitation; a metal can also form in a redox reaction.
- Visual: precipitation

### Single-replacement reaction
- Definition: One element ==replaces another element== in a compound.
- Relation: \(A+BC\rightarrow AC+B\); often involves electron transfer.
- Visual: reaction
### Oxidation and reduction
- Definition: ==Oxidation = electron loss; reduction = electron gain==.
- Key idea: Identify the species that loses electrons and the species that gains electrons; both occur in a redox reaction.
- Visual: redox
### Acid–carbonate reaction
- Definition: An acid reacts with carbonate to ==produce carbon dioxide and water==.
- Relation: \(2H^++CO_3^{2-}\rightarrow CO_2+H_2O\).
- Key idea: Bubbles indicate a gas; identify \(CO_2\) and explain its formation.
- Visual: reaction
### Catalyst
- Definition: Provides a pathway with ==lower activation energy== and is regenerated overall.
- Relation: Reaction rate changes; net reaction and product identities do not.
- Visual: energy-barrier
### Expected evidence
- Definition: An observation ==predicted by a model==, not a result that was actually measured.
- Key idea: Label evidence as observed or expected when making a prediction.
- Visual: observation
