### Ionic bonding
- Definition: Electrostatic attraction between ==oppositely charged ions== in an extended lattice.
- Key idea: Ionic solids are not separate molecules; the formula is the simplest neutral ion ratio.
- Visual: ionic-lattice
### Covalent bond
- Definition: A bond formed when atoms ==share one or more electron pairs==.
- Key idea: Covalent substances can be discrete molecules or extended networks.
- Visual: covalent-bond
### Lewis structure
- Definition: A drawing of valence electrons as ==bonds and lone pairs==.
- Key idea: It shows connectivity and electrons; by itself it does not show 3D molecular shape.
- Visual: lewis
### Chemical nomenclature
- Definition: Naming substances from their ==composition and bonding type==.
- Relation: Ionic formula: total charge \(=0\). Molecular compound: prefixes give atom counts.
- Visual: ratio
### Formal charge
- Definition: A ==bookkeeping charge== assigned to an atom in a Lewis structure.
- Relation: \(FC=V-N-\frac{B}{2}\), where \(V\)=valence electrons, \(N\)=nonbonding electrons, \(B\)=bonding electrons.
- Visual: lewis
### Resonance structures
- Definition: Lewis structures with the ==same atom arrangement== but different electron placement.
- Key idea: Atoms do not move between resonance forms; only electrons move.
- Visual: resonance
### Electron-domain geometry
- Definition: Arrangement of ==all electron domains== around a central atom, including bonds and lone pairs.
- Relation: Count electron domains to determine the geometry.
- Visual: geometry
### Molecular geometry
- Definition: The ==3D arrangement of bonded atoms==; lone pairs affect shape but are not named as atoms.
- Key idea: Use molecular geometry for questions about ==shape, bond angles, or molecular polarity==.
- Visual: geometry

### VSEPR model
- Definition: Predicts shape by treating electron domains as ==regions that repel one another==.
- Key idea: Lone pairs occupy more space than bonding domains and can compress bond angles.
- Visual: geometry
### Hybridization
- Definition: A model describing ==mixed atomic orbitals== at a central atom.
- Relation: If requested, match the label to the electron-domain count; do not add it unless useful.
- Visual: orbitals
