### What should an AP Chemistry written explanation include?
- **Claim:** “**[Direct answer to the question]**.”
- **Model or evidence:** “The **[data / diagram / electron configuration / particle model]** shows **[specific feature]**.”
- **Chemical reasoning:** “Because **[relevant interaction or particle change]**, **[property or process]** **[increases/decreases/occurs]**.”
- **Connection:** “This explains **[the requested observation, comparison, or prediction]**.”
- ✓ Include only the chemistry needed for the prompt; a term without its causal link is not a complete explanation.

### Which ideas are required for each kind of explanation?
| If the question asks about… | Include these steps or terms | Add only when the prompt involves… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A periodic trend or electron removal | State the direction; compare electron distance and shielding; connect effective nuclear charge to ==Coulombic attraction==; link the attraction to the property. | Subshell stability or electron-pair repulsion when the data show an ionization-energy exception. |
| Molecular shape or polarity | Draw or read the Lewis structure; state the 3-D molecular geometry; identify polar bonds and the direction of their dipoles; explain whether the dipoles cancel. | Lone-pair geometry or hybridization when specifically asked or needed to establish shape. |
| A physical-property comparison | Identify the relevant particle structure and intermolecular force; connect its relative strength to the property. | Hydrogen bonding, ion–dipole attraction, or molecular surface area when present in the structures. |
| A reaction observation or prediction | Identify the reactants and products at the particle level; name the reaction type if asked or useful; connect the particle change to the evidence. | Electron transfer, acid–base particles, solubility, activation energy, or a catalyst when that mechanism explains the observation. |
| A calculation or data graph | Show the relationship, substitute values with units, and interpret the result in the requested chemical context. | A model-based explanation when the prompt asks why the result has that value or pattern. |
- ✓ **Coulombic attraction is not a universal keyword.** Use it when the cause is electrostatic attraction between charged particles, especially nucleus–electron comparisons.
- ✓ **Molecular structure is required when shape or molecular polarity matters.** Describe the 3-D arrangement and dipole cancellation; do not insert it into an atomic-trend answer.

### How do you write a claim–evidence–reasoning response from a graph or model?
- “**[Claim answering the prompt]**.”
- “The **[graph/model/table]** shows **[specific value, pattern, peak, or structural feature]**.”
- “At the particle level, **[identify the relevant particles and their arrangement or interaction]**.”
- “Because **[chemical principle]**, **[particle-level change]** causes **[property or observed result]**.”
- “Therefore, the evidence supports **[claim]**.”
- ✓ Quote or calculate the relevant evidence; then explain why it supports the claim instead of repeating the claim.
