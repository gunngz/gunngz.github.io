### Polar bond
- Definition: A covalent bond with ==unequal sharing== because the atoms have different electronegativities.
- Relation: Dipole points toward the more electronegative atom: \(\delta^+\to\delta^-\).
- Visual: dipole
### Molecular polarity
- Definition: The ==net charge distribution== from a molecule's bond dipoles and 3D structure.
- Key idea: Polar bonds can cancel in a symmetric molecule.
- Visual: molecular-dipole
### Dipole moment
- Definition: A measure of the ==separation of positive and negative charge==.
- Relation: \(\vec\mu_{\mathrm{net}}=\sum_i\vec\mu_i\); direction matters.
- Visual: dipole
### London dispersion force
- Definition: Attraction from ==temporary and induced dipoles==.
- Key idea: Present between all atoms and molecules; generally stronger for more polarizable electron clouds.
- Visual: dispersion
### Dipole–dipole attraction
- Definition: Attraction between ==partial positive and partial negative== ends of neighboring polar molecules.
- Visual: dipole-pair
### Hydrogen bonding
- Definition: Strong dipole–dipole attraction when ==H bonded to N, O, or F== interacts with a lone pair on N, O, or F.
- Key idea: Hydrogen alone is not enough; check the bonded atom and the lone-pair atom.
- Visual: hydrogen-bond
### Ion–dipole attraction
- Definition: Attraction between ==an ion and the partial charges== of a polar molecule.
- Key idea: Important when ionic substances interact with polar solvents such as water.
- Visual: ion-dipole
### Intermolecular forces (IMFs)
- Definition: Attractions between ==separate particles==, distinct from bonds within a molecule or ionic lattice.
- Relation: For comparable substances, stronger IMFs generally mean boiling point \(\uparrow\), viscosity \(\uparrow\), vapor pressure \(\downarrow\).
- Visual: dispersion
### Solubility and polarity
- Definition: Dissolution is favored when new ==solute–solvent attractions== compensate for attractions in the original substances.
- Key idea: Compare the specific particles and attractions; “like dissolves like” is only a starting clue.
- Visual: solvation
