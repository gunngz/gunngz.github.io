### Polar bond
- Definition: A covalent bond with ==unequal sharing== because the atoms have different electronegativities.
- Relation: The bond dipole points toward the more electronegative atom, from partial positive to partial negative.
- Example: In H–Cl, the shared electrons spend more time near chlorine.
- Visual: dipole

### Molecular polarity
- Definition: The ==overall distribution of charge== from bond dipoles and three-dimensional molecular shape.
- Example: Carbon dioxide has polar bonds, but their dipoles cancel; water's bent shape leaves a net dipole.
- Key idea: Polar bonds can cancel in a symmetric molecule.
- Visual: tetrahedral-3d

### Dipole moment
- Definition: A measure of the ==separation of positive and negative charge==.
- Relation: The molecular dipole is the vector sum of all bond dipoles; direction matters.
- Visual: dipole

### Solubility and polarity
- Definition: Dissolution is favored when new ==solute–solvent attractions== compensate for attractions in the original substances.
- Example: Water molecules surround dissolved sodium and chloride ions using their partial charges.
- Key idea: Compare the actual particles and attractions; “like dissolves like” is only a starting clue.
- Visual: solvation
