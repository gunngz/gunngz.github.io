### Coulombic attraction
- The electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged particles, such as the nucleus and an electron.
- Attraction increases with greater charge and decreases as the distance between particles increases.

### Effective nuclear charge
- The net positive charge experienced by an electron after shielding by other electrons is considered.
- Across a period, nuclear charge increases while core-electron shielding changes little, so the effective nuclear charge generally increases.

### Shielding
- Repulsion from inner electrons reduces the nucleus's attraction for an outer electron.
- Down a group, added inner shells increase shielding and place valence electrons farther from the nucleus.

### Atomic radius
- A measure of atomic size.
- Across a period it generally decreases; down a group it generally increases.

### First ionization energy
- The energy required to remove the first electron from a gaseous atom.
- A stronger attraction between the nucleus and the valence electron generally means a higher ionization energy.

### Electronegativity
- A measure of how strongly an atom attracts shared electrons in a chemical bond.
- It generally increases across a period and decreases down a group.

### Periodic-trend explanation
- A complete explanation names the property, compares the relevant electron distance and shielding, and connects these to ==Coulombic attraction==.
- ✓ State the direction of the trend and explain the particle-level cause; a memorized arrow alone is not a justification.

### Ion charge and valence electrons
- Typical main-group ion charges are related to the number of valence electrons and the tendency to reach a stable electron configuration.
- Use charge balance in an ionic formula to determine an unknown ion charge; do not infer a unique charge when the formula or element allows multiple possibilities.
