### Coulombic attraction
- Definition: Electrostatic attraction between ==opposite charges==, such as the nucleus and an electron.
- Relation: \(F_C\propto\frac{|q_1q_2|}{r^2}\); charge magnitude \(\uparrow\) or distance \(r\downarrow\) \(\Rightarrow\) attraction \(\uparrow\).
- Visual: coulomb
### Effective nuclear charge
- Definition: ==Net positive charge== experienced by an electron after shielding.
- Relation: \(Z_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx Z-S\); across a period, \(Z\) rises while core shielding changes little.
- Visual: coulomb
### Shielding
- Definition: Core electrons repel outer electrons and ==reduce their attraction to the nucleus==.
- Relation: Down a group: more inner shells \(\Rightarrow\) shielding \(\uparrow\), distance \(\uparrow\).
- Visual: coulomb
### Atomic radius
- Definition: A measure of ==atomic size==.
- Relation: Across a period: radius generally \(\downarrow\). Down a group: radius generally \(\uparrow\).
- Visual: periodic-trend
### First ionization energy
- Definition: Energy required to ==remove the first electron== from a gaseous atom.
- Relation: \(X(g)\to X^+(g)+e^-\); stronger attraction generally \(\Rightarrow\) higher ionization energy.
- Visual: periodic-trend
### Electronegativity
- Definition: An atom's ==attraction for shared electrons== in a bond.
- Relation: Generally \(\uparrow\) across a period and \(\downarrow\) down a group.
- Visual: periodic-trend
### Periodic-trend explanation
- Definition: Explain a trend by connecting electron distance and shielding to ==Coulombic attraction==.
- Key idea: State the direction, then give the particle-level cause; an arrow alone is not an explanation.
- Visual: coulomb

### Ion charge and valence electrons
- Definition: Main-group atoms ==gain or lose electrons== toward a stable outer-shell configuration.
- Relation: Ion charge \(=Z-\#e^-\); ionic formula total charge \(=0\).
- Key idea: Use the formula or given ion to determine charge; some elements have more than one possible ion charge.
- Visual: ion
