### Ground-state electron configuration
- Definition: The electron arrangement with the ==lowest total energy== for that atom or ion.
- Relation: Check that the configuration contains the correct total \(\#e^-\).
- Visual: orbitals

### Aufbau principle
- Definition: Electrons occupy available orbitals in order of ==increasing energy==.
- Relation: \(1s<2s<2p<3s<3p<4s<3d\).
- Visual: orbitals
- Animation: aufbau

### Pauli exclusion principle
- Definition: One orbital holds at most two electrons with ==opposite spins==.
- Visual: paired-orbital

### Hund's rule
- Definition: In equal-energy orbitals, place ==one electron in each orbital before pairing==; unpaired spins are parallel.
- Visual: hund-orbitals
### Valence electrons
- Definition: Electrons in the ==outermost occupied shell== of a main-group atom.
- Key idea: Valence electrons help predict common ion charges and bonding.
- Visual: atom
### Ion electron configuration
- Definition: ==Cations lose electrons; anions gain electrons==.
- Relation: For transition-metal cations, remove electrons from the ==highest principal energy level first== (for example, \(4s\) before \(3d\)).
- Visual: ion
### Photoelectron spectroscopy (PES)
- Definition: Measures the ==energy needed to remove electrons== from atoms or ions.
- Relation: Peak position \(\to\) binding energy; peak area \(\to\) electron count.
- Visual: spectrum
### Binding energy
- Definition: ==Energy required to remove an electron== from an atom or ion.
- Relation: Greater binding energy \(\Rightarrow\) electron held more strongly. Check the axis direction.
- Visual: spectrum
### Ground state and excited state
- Definition: Ground state has the ==lowest possible energy==; excited state has the same \(\#e^-\) with at least one electron at higher energy.
- Visual: energy-levels
