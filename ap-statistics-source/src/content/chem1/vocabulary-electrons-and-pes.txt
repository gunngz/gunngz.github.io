### Ground-state electron configuration
- Definition: The electron arrangement with the ==lowest total energy== for that atom or ion.
- Relation: The configuration must contain the correct total number of electrons.
- Example: Magnesium has \(1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\).
- Visual: orbitals

### Aufbau principle
- Definition: Electrons occupy available orbitals in order of ==increasing energy==.
- Relation: Fill \(1s\), then \(2s\), then the three \(2p\) orbitals, then \(3s\), then the three \(3p\) orbitals.
- Example: Magnesium's first twelve electrons fill \(1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\).
- Visual: orbitals
- Animation: aufbau

### Pauli exclusion principle
- Definition: One orbital holds at most two electrons with ==opposite spins==.
- Example: A filled orbital is drawn with one upward arrow and one downward arrow.
- Visual: paired-orbital

### Hund's rule
- Definition: In equal-energy orbitals, place ==one electron in each orbital before pairing==; unpaired spins are parallel.
- Example: Four electrons in three equal-energy orbitals are drawn as one pair and two single electrons.
- Visual: hund-orbitals

### Valence electrons
- Definition: Electrons in the ==outermost occupied shell== of a main-group atom.
- Example: Magnesium has two valence electrons in its third shell.
- Key idea: Valence electrons help predict common ion charges and bonding.
- Visual: atom

### Ion electron configuration
- Definition: ==Cations lose electrons; anions gain electrons==.
- Relation: For transition-metal cations, remove electrons from the highest principal energy level first; for example, remove the \(4s\) electrons before the \(3d\) electrons.
- Example: Iron forms \(\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}\) by losing its two \(4s\) electrons.
- Visual: ion

### Photoelectron spectroscopy
- Definition: Measures the ==energy needed to remove electrons== from atoms or ions.
- Relation: Peak position represents binding energy; relative peak area represents the number of electrons.
- Example: A peak with area three times another represents three times as many electrons.
- Visual: photoelectron-spectrum

### Binding energy
- Definition: ==Energy required to remove an electron== from an atom or ion.
- Relation: Greater binding energy means the electron is held more strongly; check which direction the graph's energy axis increases.
- Visual: photoelectron-spectrum

### Ground state and excited state
- Definition: A ground state has the ==lowest possible energy==; an excited state has the same number of electrons with at least one electron at higher energy.
- Example: Moving one electron to a higher orbital creates an excited state without changing the atom's electron count.
- Visual: energy-levels
