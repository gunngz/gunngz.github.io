### Atomic number
- Definition: The number of ==protons in the nucleus==; it identifies the element.
- Relation: #protons = atomic number
- Relation: In a neutral atom, #e− = #protons.
- Visual: atom

### Mass number
- Definition: The total number of ==protons and neutrons== in one isotope.
- Relation: \(\#\mathrm{neutrons}=\mathrm{mass\ number}-\mathrm{atomic\ number}\).
- Visual: mass-number

### Isotope
- Definition: Atoms of one element with the same number of protons but ==different numbers of neutrons==.
- Example: Hydrogen-1, hydrogen-2, and hydrogen-3 each have one proton; they have zero, one, and two neutrons.
- Visual: isotopes

### Average atomic mass
- Definition: The ==weighted average== of an element's isotope masses.
- Relation: Add each \(\mathrm{isotope\ mass}\times\mathrm{fractional\ abundance}\); all fractional abundances add to 1.
- Example: If an isotope has 75% abundance, use 0.75 as its fractional abundance.
- Visual: weighted-mass

### Mass spectrum
- Definition: A graph of ions by ==mass-to-charge ratio== and relative abundance.
- Relation: Peak position identifies an isotope's mass; relative peak height represents relative abundance.
- Visual: mass-spectrum

### Mole
- Definition: An amount containing ==6.022\times10^{23} specified particles==.
- Example: Name the particles being counted: atoms, molecules, ions, or formula units.
- Visual: particles

### Molar mass
- Definition: The ==mass of one mole== of a substance.
- Relation: Add the atomic masses using every subscript in the chemical formula; the unit is grams per mole.
- Example: One mole of \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) contains two moles of hydrogen atoms and one mole of oxygen atoms.
- Visual: particles

### Percent composition
- Definition: The ==mass percentage== of an element in a sample.
- Relation: \(\%\,\mathrm{element}=\frac{\mathrm{mass\ of\ element}}{\mathrm{mass\ of\ sample}}\times100\%\).
- Example: Water always has the same mass percentages of hydrogen and oxygen; salt water can vary.
- Visual: composition

### Empirical formula
- Definition: The ==lowest whole-number ratio== of atoms or ions in a substance.
- Relation: An ionic formula has total charge \(=0\).
- Example: The empirical formula for \(\mathrm{H_2O_2}\) is \(\mathrm{HO}\).
- Visual: ratio

### Molecular formula
- Definition: The ==actual number of each atom== in one molecule.
- Relation: Its subscripts are a whole-number multiple of the empirical-formula subscripts.
- Example: \(\mathrm{H_2O_2}\) has molecular formula \(\mathrm{H_2O_2}\) and empirical formula \(\mathrm{HO}\).
- Visual: ratio
