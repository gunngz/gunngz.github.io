### Atomic number
- Definition: The number of ==protons in the nucleus==; it identifies the element.
- Relation: \(Z=\#p^+\); neutral atom: \(\#e^-=Z\).
- Visual: atom

### Mass number
- Definition: The total number of ==protons and neutrons== in one isotope.
- Relation: \(A=\#p^++\#n^0\); \(\#n^0=A-Z\).
- Visual: atom
### Isotope
- Definition: Atoms with the same \(Z\) but ==different numbers of neutrons==.
- Relation: Same element \(\Rightarrow\) same proton count; isotope mass can differ.
- Visual: isotopes

### Average atomic mass
- Definition: The ==weighted average== of an element's isotope masses.
- Relation: \(\bar m=\sum_i f_i m_i\); \(\sum_i f_i=1\) (fractional abundances).
- Visual: weighted-mass

### Mass spectrum
- Definition: Ion signal plotted against ==mass-to-charge ratio==, \(m/z\).
- Relation: Peak position \(\to\) isotope mass; relative peak height \(\to\) relative abundance.
- Visual: spectrum
### Mole
- Definition: An amount containing ==Avogadro's number== of specified particles.
- Relation: \(1\ \mathrm{mol}=6.022\times10^{23}\) particles; name them: atoms, molecules, ions, or formula units.
- Visual: particles
### Molar mass
- Definition: The ==mass of one mole== of a substance.
- Relation: Unit: \(\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}\); sum atomic masses using every formula subscript.
- Visual: particles
### Percent composition
- Definition: The ==mass percentage== of an element in a sample.
- Relation: \(\%X=\frac{m_X}{m_{\mathrm{sample}}}\times100\%\).
- Key idea: A compound has fixed composition; a mixture can vary.
- Visual: composition
### Empirical formula
- Definition: The ==lowest whole-number ratio== of atoms or ions.
- Relation: For an ionic formula, total charge \(=0\).
- Visual: ratio

### Molecular formula
- Definition: The ==actual number of each atom== in one molecule.
- Relation: Molecular formula \(=n\times\) empirical formula, where \(n\) is a whole number.
- Visual: ratio
