### Frequency
- ==The number of observations in a category==.

### Relative frequency
- ==The proportion of observations in a category==: **category frequency / total frequency**.
- Relative frequencies across all categories ==sum to 1, or 100%==.

### Bar chart
- A graph for categorical data with ==separate bars== whose heights show counts or proportions.
- ✓ Gaps separate the categories; bar width has no numerical meaning.

### Pie chart
- A circle divided into slices whose areas or angles represent categories' parts of one whole.
- ✓ Use a pie chart only when the categories form ==parts of the same whole==.

### Segmented bar chart
- A bar divided into segments showing the ==conditional proportions== for one group.
- Equal-length bars make group proportions easier to compare.

### Two-way table
- A table that counts observations for combinations of two categorical variables.

### Conditional distribution
- The distribution of one categorical variable within a specified category of another variable.
- ✓ Use row or column percentages with the relevant group as the denominator when comparing groups.

### Association between categorical variables
- The ==conditional distributions differ== across categories of the other variable.
- A difference in sample proportions describes an association; study design determines what conclusions are justified.
