### Normal distribution
- A ==symmetric, bell-shaped distribution== described by a mean and standard deviation.

### Standard Normal distribution
- The Normal distribution with mean 0 and standard deviation 1.

### Normal probability plot (NPP)
- A plot that compares ordered data with values expected from a Normal distribution.
- ✓ A roughly straight pattern supports an approximately Normal model; clear curvature or strong departures from a line do not.

### normalcdf
- A calculator function that finds the ==area (probability)== between specified lower and upper bounds under a Normal curve.
- ✓ For a continuous Normal variable, probability is area; do not use `normalpdf` to find probability.

### inverseNorm
- A calculator function that converts a ==cumulative area or percentile== into the corresponding z-score or data value.

### Empirical rule
- For an approximately Normal distribution, about ==68%, 95%, and 99.7%== of values lie within 1, 2, and 3 standard deviations of the mean, respectively.
