# Study Pages: AP Statistics, AP Chemistry, U.S. History, and USACO

This is a React/Vite study guide. The app owns the layout and interactions; study material lives in plain-text `.txt` files under `src/content/`. Vite discovers and groups those files automatically.

## Add or extend content

Create a `.txt` file inside `src/content/`. Use this frontmatter block, then add one `###` heading per card:

    ---
    unit: u1p1
    kind: vocabulary
    category: Study Basics
    ---

    ### A term or question
    - A separate point on each line.
    - ✓ **Key idea:** An explanation or distinction.

Use `unit: u1p1`, `unit: u1p2`, or `unit: u2p1` for Statistics; `unit: chem1` or `unit: chem2` for Chemistry; `unit: ushist3` for U.S. History Unit 3; and `unit: usaco` with `kind: patterns` for USACO. Statistics supports vocabulary, answer templates, and (for Unit 2 Part 1) calculations. Chemistry supports answer templates and (for Unit 2) lab review. U.S. History supports vocabulary and check-for-understanding questions. USACO contains problem-solving patterns. Add a category name. New categories are added to the horizontal navigation automatically. Unit 1 Part 2 cards may include `{days=1,3}` at the end of the heading; Unit 2 Part 1 cards may include `{days=1,3}` for its five-day filter.

Use `==important wording==` to highlight an idea, `**[placeholders]**` for fill-in fields, and `\( ... \)` / `\[ ... \]` for LaTeX math. MathJax renders formulas. Use a Markdown table for compact comparisons.

Statistics Unit 2 has a separate **Proportion & Percentage** tab (`kind: number-format`). It reminds students to write proportions as decimals and percentages with `%`, and to draw a labeled, shaded diagram for every Normal-model problem. This tab is independent of the day filter.
Every card in Unit 1's **Normal Models** vocabulary and answer-template pages includes its corresponding sketch. `scripts/draw_normal_models.py` generates the eight reusable SVGs from the Normal density; probabilities and percentiles use Python's `statistics.NormalDist`. Keep curves normalized, distinguish shaded area from height, and match each sketch to its card.

Chemistry Unit 2 separates the two experiments: **Titration** uses `kind: lab-review`, and **Gas Lab Analysis** uses `kind: gas-lab`. General answer templates remain under `kind: template`.

The separate **Z Curves** view uses `kind: z-curves` for real-gas explanations.
Its `{{z-curve-diagram}}` card displays annotated CH4, CO2, and H2 model curves with selectable regions and a MathJax equation. The SVG is generated by `scripts/draw_z_curve.py` using Matplotlib. The `{{z-sentence-template}}` card has five header choices; `{{z-gas-applications}}` combines the three gas explanations into one card. They use one fixed sentence frame, with only bracketed slot values changing.
The separate **Maxwell–Boltzmann** view uses `kind: maxwell-boltzmann`. Its `{{maxwell-boltzmann-diagram}}` card switches between temperature and molar-mass comparisons using the normalized curves generated by `scripts/draw_maxwell_boltzmann.py`.
The same card also switches the horizontal axis between speed and translational kinetic energy. `scripts/draw_mb_extensions.py` generates the energy curves and four speed comparisons beside the curve-shape table.
The `{{mb-sentence-template}}` card has four header choices and shares the selected horizontal axis with the diagram. Its fixed wording is identical across all Speed and energy examples; only slot values change. Template content lives in `src/content-data/`, formatting in `template-format.js`, and interaction in `TemplateCard.jsx`. All example values are indexed by search.
The `{{gas-experiment-template}}` card combines the general four-part explanation with two experiment buttons, the filled English sentence, the essential causal link, and the selected relations. The `{{acid-confirmation-template}}` card switches among four identity-confirmation methods in one Method → Evidence → Limitation frame. A selected button can be clicked again to return to the generic guide or placeholders. Shared instructions live in `guide`/`extraFrames`; equations are kept with the corresponding choice.

**Test Feedback** uses `kind: test-feedback` for the supplied Unit 2 exam scoring guide, translated into English. Its four category pages cover multiple-choice answers and FRQ 1–2, 3–4, and 5–6. Keep the point values, review-priority marks, and provisional FRQ 5(a) mark when updating this content.
Each exam card includes its original question and required context, followed by `<!-- answer -->` and the matching answer/scoring points. The separator keeps the question visible when its answer is blurred. Both question and answer are searchable. Preserve the original options, quantities, assumptions, and referenced figures when updating questions.
Wrap a question image and its caption in `<!-- figure -->` / `<!-- /figure -->` to place it beside the question on wide screens. Put `<!-- choices -->` before multiple-choice options so they sit below the question/figure row and remain visible when the answer is masked. Question figures fit within 320px height, with images kept in proportion and no forced raster upscaling. Standalone diagrams have a 560px height limit; small screens stack question and figure.
Worked chemistry calculations carry units and species through dimensional-analysis conversion factors, including volume conversions and stoichiometric ratios. Keep intermediate values unrounded and preserve the original scoring-point structure.
Append `{priority}` to a card heading to show a star before its title; use `{priority=provisional}` for an unconfirmed mark. Chemistry's primary tabs stay on a horizontally scrolling rail, with search outside the rail.

Statistics, Chemistry, U.S. History, and USACO have separate routes and content. Chemistry opens directly to its answer templates; USACO opens to its patterns; Statistics and U.S. History have separate content modes. The pages use warm light colors and keep each category as its own horizontal-navigation page. The centered content width preserves the side margins; on wide screens, the collapsible unit sidebar uses the left margin without shifting the cards. Individual and section-wide eye icons blur answers without removing them.

## Run locally

    npm install
    npm run dev

Create a production build with `npm run build`. The personal site publishes Statistics at `/ap-statistics/`, Chemistry at `/ap-chemistry/`, U.S. History at `/us-history/`, and USACO at `/usaco/`. To add material, add a `.txt` file here, sync the source into `study-material-source/`, and push; the Pages workflow rebuilds the guides without React-component edits for text-only additions.
