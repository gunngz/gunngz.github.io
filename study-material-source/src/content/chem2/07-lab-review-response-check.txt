### General Chemical Explanation

> [Substance] has ==[structural feature]==, allowing it to ==[donate \(\mathrm{H^+}\) / accept \(\mathrm{H^+}\) / produce ions]==. These particles react with ==[other particles]== to form ==[products]==. Therefore, ==[observation or conclusion]==.

| Step | What must be explained? | Common expressions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structure | What structural feature does the substance have? | contains two ionizable H atoms / contains \(\mathrm{-COOH}\) groups |
| Particles | What particles are produced or transferred? | donates \(\mathrm{H^+}\) / produces \(\mathrm{OH^-}\) / dissociates into ions |
| Reaction | Which particles react with each other? | \(\mathrm{H^+}\) reacts with \(\mathrm{OH^-}\) |
| Products | What is formed? | forms water / produces a salt |
| Result | What does the reaction show? | complete neutralization / 1:2 mole ratio |

### Acid–Base / Neutralization

> The ==acid donates \(\mathrm{H^+}\)==, while the ==base accepts \(\mathrm{H^+}\)==. The \(\mathrm{H^+}\) ==reacts with \(\mathrm{OH^-}\) to form water==, and the ==remaining ions form a salt==. Based on the balanced equation, the ==mole ratio is [ratio]==.

\[
\mathrm{H^+ + OH^- \rightarrow H_2O}
\]

### Diprotic Acid / Structure

> The acid contains ==two ionizable H atoms==, meaning it can donate ==two \(\mathrm{H^+}\) ions per molecule==. Each \(\mathrm{H^+}\) ==reacts with one \(\mathrm{OH^-}\) to form water==. Therefore, the acid reacts with \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) in a ==1:2 mole ratio==.
