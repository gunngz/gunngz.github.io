### Chemical reactions
| Step | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| Claim | State the claim and identify the particle type (e.g., acid) or reaction type (e.g., redox). |
| Particle | What are the particles? (e.g., \(\mathrm{NaOH}\))<br>For example: \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) reacts with \(\mathrm{HCl}\). |
| Reasoning | State what the reaction produces. |
| Evidence | Support the claim with the observed evidence. |
| Distinction | Unlike [comparison reaction], this reaction does not [distinguishing feature]. |
