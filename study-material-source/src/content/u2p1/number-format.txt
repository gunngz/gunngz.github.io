### Proportion vs. percentage
| Quantity | Write as | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proportion | ==A decimal== | 0.25 |
| Percentage | ==A number followed by %== | 25% |

### Normal distribution: always draw a diagram
- For ==every Normal distribution problem==, ==draw a diagram==.
- Label the mean, standard deviation, and relevant values.
- ==Shade the requested probability== or the area corresponding to the percentile.
