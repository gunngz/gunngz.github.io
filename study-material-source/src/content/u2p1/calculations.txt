### Relative frequencies {days=1}
For a two-way table:

\[\text{joint relative frequency}=\frac{\text{cell count}}{n}\]

\[\text{marginal relative frequency}=\frac{\text{row or column total}}{n}\]

\[\text{conditional relative frequency}=\frac{\text{cell count}}{\text{total for the given group}}\]

### Segmented bar chart and mosaic plot {days=1}
To construct a segmented bar chart:

1. Identify the ==explanatory== and ==response== variables.
2. Draw and label the axes: explanatory-variable categories on the horizontal axis; proportions or percentages on the vertical axis.
3. Scale the vertical axis from 0% to 100% (or 0 to 1).
4. Draw equal-width 100% bars with gaps between them, one for each explanatory-variable group.
5. Use the response-variable conditional relative frequencies to divide each bar into segments.
6. Add a key to identify the response categories.

For a mosaic plot, make each bar's width proportional to the size of its explanatory-variable group and remove the gaps between bars.

### Simulation estimates {days=2}
For the observed proportion of a single outcome:

\[\widehat{P}(A)=\frac{\text{number of simulated successes}}{\text{number of trials}}\]

For a claim test, count complete ==repetitions== that match the observed result or are more extreme:

\[\widehat{P}(\text{result at least as extreme})=\frac{\text{repetitions at least as extreme}}{\text{total repetitions}}\]

- Homework source: U2 HW #2, §2B #9(c), 11(c), 12(c).

### Probability rules {days=3,4}
\[0\leq P(A)\leq 1\]

\[\sum P(\text{outcome})=1\]

For equally likely outcomes:

\[P(A)=\frac{\text{number of outcomes in }A}{\text{number of outcomes in }S}\]

\[P(A^c)=1-P(A)\]

\[P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\]

For mutually exclusive events:

\[P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)\]

![Venn diagram showing A only, the overlap A intersection B, and B only for the addition rule.](./diagrams/u2p1-venn.svg)

### Conditional probability {days=4,5}
\[P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}=\frac{\text{count in both }A\text{ and }B}{\text{count in }B}\]

PPT source: U2P1 Day 4.

For diagnostic tests, let \(D\) mean “has the condition”:

\[\text{false positive rate}=P(+\mid D^c)\]

\[\text{false negative rate}=P(-\mid D)\]

Do not confuse \(P(D^c\mid +)\) with \(P(+\mid D^c)\); they condition on different events.

### Independence {days=4,5}
\[P(A\mid B)=P(A)\]

\[P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)\]

### General multiplication rule {days=4,5}
\[P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B\mid A)=P(B)P(A\mid B)\]

- Homework source: U2 HW #5, §2D #17–18; Day 5 Practice #14–15.

### Two draws without replacement {days=3,4,5}
For two successes from \(K\) success items among \(N\) items:

\[P(\text{both successes})=\frac{K}{N}\cdot\frac{K-1}{N-1}\]

- Homework source: Probability Level-Up, Blue Belt #1; Day 5 Practice #14.

### At least one {days=3,5}
\[P(\text{at least one})=1-P(\text{none})\]

For \(n\) ==independent attempts== with the same success probability \(p\):

\[P(\text{at least one success})=1-(1-p)^n\]

- Homework source: Probability Level-Up, Orange Belt #1(f) and Green Belt #1.

### Diagnostic test probabilities {days=5}
Let \(D\) mean “has the condition.”

\[P(-\mid D)=1-\text{sensitivity}\]

\[P(+\mid D^c)=1-\text{specificity}\]

\[P(D\mid +)=\frac{P(D)P(+\mid D)}{P(D)P(+\mid D)+P(D^c)P(+\mid D^c)}\]

- Homework source: U2 HW #5, §2D #25–26.

### Tree diagrams and total probability {days=4,5}
- Path: multiply. Event: add matching paths.

\[P(B)=P(A)P(B\mid A)+P(A^c)P(B\mid A^c)\]

\[P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A)P(B\mid A)}{P(B)}\]

- PPT source: U2P1 Day 4.
