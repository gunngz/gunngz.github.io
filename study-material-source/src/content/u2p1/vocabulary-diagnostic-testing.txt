### False positive {days=4,5}
- A test indicates that a condition is present when the condition is actually absent.
- For condition \(D\) and a positive result \(+\), this is \(P(+\mid D^c)\).

### False negative {days=4,5}
- A test indicates that a condition is absent when the condition is actually present.
- For condition \(D\), this is \(P(-\mid D)\).
- \(P(D^c\mid +)\) is a different conditional probability: it asks whether the condition is absent given a positive result.
