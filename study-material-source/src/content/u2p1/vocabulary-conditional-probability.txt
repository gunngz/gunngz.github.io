### Conditional probability {days=4,5}
- The probability of an event after another event is known to have occurred.
- The condition reduces the sample space to the outcomes in that condition; \(P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}\), when \(P(B)>0\).

### Independent events {days=4,5}
- Events are independent when knowing that one occurred does not change the probability of the other.
- Check either \(P(A\mid B)=P(A)\) or \(P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)\).

### Tree diagram {days=4,5}
- A diagram of sequential events; each branch shows a conditional probability and each path shows one sequence of outcomes.
- Multiply probabilities along a path; add the probabilities of disjoint paths that meet the event of interest.
