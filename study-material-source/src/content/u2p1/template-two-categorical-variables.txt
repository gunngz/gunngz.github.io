### Describe an association {days=1}
“There is a ==clear association== between **[explanatory variable]** and **[response variable]** in this ==random sample==. Knowing **[explanatory variable]** ==helps us predict== **[response variable]**. **[Group 1]** were most likely to **[response]** (about **[percent]%**), followed by **[Group 2]** (about **[percent]%**), and then **[Group 3]** (about **[percent]%**).”

For no association: “Knowing **[explanatory variable]** does not help us predict **[response variable]**.” The ==conditional distributions are about the same across groups==.

For a population: “Is there an association between **[variable 1]** and **[variable 2]** in **[population]**?” Answering this requires ==formal inference==.

- Compare ==conditional distributions== across groups.
- PPT source: U2P1 Day 1, slides 22–27 and 34.

### Interpret a conditional percentage {days=1}
“Among **[group]**, **[percent]%** **[response]**.”

Key idea: Use the ==total for that group== as the denominator. Compare percentages within groups when the groups have different sizes.

- Homework source: U2 HW #1, §2A #1(c), 3(c) (adapted).
