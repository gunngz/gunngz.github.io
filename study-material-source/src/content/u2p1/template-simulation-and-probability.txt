### Simulation response: four steps {days=2}
1. State the ==question of interest==.
2. Plan and describe how chance will imitate one repetition; state what is recorded after each repetition.
3. Perform many repetitions.
4. Conclude based on the results.

Conclusion: “In **[number of simulations]** repetitions of **[number of trials]** each, **[number]** resulted in **[event]**. So about **[percent]%** of the time, **[event]** would occur if **[chance model]** were true. **[Observed result]** seems **[likely/unlikely]** to happen by chance.”

- PPT source: U2P1 Day 2, slides 3–4.

### Interpret a probability {days=2}
“In a very large number of **[trials]**, about **[probability × 100]%** of outcomes would be **[event]**. A sample of **[sample size]** trials will not necessarily contain exactly **[expected count]** occurrences because probability describes long-run behavior.”

- PPT source: U2P1 Day 2, slides 11–13.
