### Question of interest {days=2}
“Can **[observed result]** happen by chance?”

PPT example: “Can seniors win 8 out of 10 Night Rallies by chance?”

- PPT source: U2P1 Day 2, slides 3–4.

### Simulation response {days=2}
1. State the ==question of interest==.
2. Plan and describe how to use chance to imitate ==one repetition==. State what will be recorded after each repetition.
3. Perform ==many repetitions==.
4. Conclude based on the results.

Conclusion: “In **[number of repetitions]** repetitions of **[number of trials]** each, **[number]** resulted in **[event]**. So about **[percent]%** of the time, **[event]** would occur if **[chance model]** were true. **[Observed result]** seems **[likely/unlikely]** to happen by chance.”

- PPT source: U2P1 Day 2, slides 3–4.

### Interpret a probability {days=2}
“If you take a ==very large random sample== of **[population]**, approximately **[probability × 100]%** of them will **[event]**.”

“Probably not. With only **[sample size]** randomly selected **[population members]**, the number who **[event]** is likely to differ from **[expected count]**.”

- Probability describes the proportion of times an outcome occurs in a ==very long series of trials==; it does not promise an exact count in a small sample.

- PPT source: U2P1 Day 2, slides 11–13.
