### Transform the mean, variance, and standard deviation
\[Y=a+bX\]

\[\mu_Y=a+b\mu_X\]

\[\sigma_Y^2=b^2\sigma_X^2\]

\[\sigma_Y=|b|\sigma_X\]

- In the investment example, \(X\) is profit in millions of dollars with \(\mu_X=3.00\) and \(\sigma_X=\sqrt{6.35}\). A fixed fee of $0.20 million and a 10% fee on profits leave \(Y=0.9X-0.2\).

\[\mu_Y=0.9(3.00)-0.2=2.50\text{ million dollars}\]

\[\sigma_Y=0.9\sqrt{6.35}\approx2.27\text{ million dollars}\]

- The fixed fee shifts every result by the same amount. The 0.9 multiplier scales the spread.
