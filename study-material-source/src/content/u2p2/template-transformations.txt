### Find the mean and spread after a linear transformation
1. Write \(Y=a+bX\) in context. Identify the fixed adjustment \(a\) and multiplier \(b\).
2. “The mean of **[new quantity]** is \(a+b\mu_X\), or **[value and units]**.”
3. “The standard deviation of **[new quantity]** is \(|b|\sigma_X\), or **[value and units]**.”
- The fixed adjustment changes the mean. Only the scale factor changes the standard deviation. If the question asks for variance, scale it by \(b^2\).
