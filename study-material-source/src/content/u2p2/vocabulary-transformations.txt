### Linear transformation of a random variable
- \(Y=a+bX\) adds a fixed amount \(a\) and scales each value by \(b\).
- Adding a constant shifts the center. Scaling by \(b\) multiplies distances by \(|b|\).
- Each original outcome carries its probability to the corresponding transformed value. If \(b=0\), all probability is at \(a\).
