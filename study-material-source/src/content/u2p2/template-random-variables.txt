### Define a random variable and build its distribution {days=1}
1. “Let \(X\) be **[the count or measurement]** for **[one repetition or unit]**.”
2. List every possible value of \(X\) and its probability. Combine outcomes that give the same value of \(X\).
3. Check that all probabilities are nonnegative and ==sum to 1==.
- **PPT source** · Day 1, slides 6–7 and 16–17.

### Find a probability from a discrete table {days=1}
1. Write the event using \(X\), such as \(P(X\ge k)\).
2. Identify the possible values that satisfy the inequality. Include the boundary for \(\le\) or \(\ge\).
3. Add the probabilities of those values and report the result in context.
- **PPT source** · Day 1, slides 7, 15–16.

### Interpret expected value {days=1}
- “In ==many repetitions== of **[process]**, the average **[quantity measured by \(X\)]** will be about **[mean] [units]**.”
- Compute the mean by multiplying each value by its probability and adding the products.
- **PPT source** · Day 1, slides 10–11, and Day 2, slide 15.

### Interpret standard deviation {days=1}
- “In ==many repetitions== of **[process]**, the **[quantity measured by \(X\)]** typically differs from its mean of **[mean] [units]** by about **[SD] [units]**.”
- Use the units of \(X\) for standard deviation and squared units for variance.
- **PPT source** · Day 1, slides 12–13, and Day 2, slide 15.
