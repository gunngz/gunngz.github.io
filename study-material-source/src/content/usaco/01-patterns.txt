### Problem-solving patterns
| Pattern | When should you consider it? |
| --- | --- |
| Forced Move | A position or choice has ==no other legal option==. |
| Greedy + Exchange Argument | There are many possible choices, and you need to prove that making a certain choice first ==will not make the result worse==. |
| Maintain an Invariant | You need to ==preserve a condition at every step== to guarantee success in the end. |
| Reverse Construction | Forward choices affect one another, so ==working backward== may be simpler. |
